## Limits

- Word order groups are a coarse proxy. Where a placeholder lands depends more on how a language orders a noun and its modifier, a possessor, or a postposition than on clause order.
- A reorder of two unnumbered placeholders of the same kind cannot be seen, so unnumbered_same rates count only numbered reorders and are a lower bound.
- Strings shared by many apps (forks, copied libraries) repeat; the repo mean and unique-pair columns show how much that matters.
- 'unsafe' rows are not all crashes: %s and %d swapped crash only where text reaches %d; otherwise the values just appear in the wrong places.
